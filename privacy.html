Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 15, 2026

Fecta is being developed as a personal-development and community-navigation platform. This policy explains the information the current website and test app may use.

Information you choose to provide

This may include your name, email address, age range, community, interests, selected direction, goals, progress, reflections, AI preference, and privacy preference.

Guest use

Guest choices and progress are stored in your browser on the device you are using. They are not automatically transferred to another device. Clearing browser storage may remove guest progress.

Account use

If you create an account, authentication and saved app data are handled through Fecta's configured account-service provider. Google sign-in may share basic account information such as your name and email after you approve it.

Location and community information

Fecta does not require precise location for the current experience. You may choose a general community so Fecta can provide more relevant local information.

AI and guidance

The current Fecta Guide uses your submitted words to suggest a starting path or next step. Fecta will not treat personal information as permanent AI memory without a clear permission choice.

How information may be used

  • Provide and personalize your Fecta path.
  • Save goals, progress, and reflections.
  • Improve usability, safety, and relevance.
  • Connect people with community information they request.

Sharing

Fecta does not publicly share private goals, reflections, or profile information by default. Community achievements should only be shared after the member approves them. Fecta does not sell personal information.

Your choices

You may use guest mode, keep AI assistance off, limit community details, or stop using the service. Account data controls, export, and deletion tools are planned as the platform develops.

Changes

This policy may change as Fecta adds features, partners, and account controls. The updated date will be revised when material changes are made.
