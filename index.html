The Fecta loop

One clear step. One meaningful review.

You do not need to solve everything at once. Fecta helps you move through four repeatable stages.

1

Check in

Notice what needs attention in your body, direction, or community.

2

Choose

Pick one meaningful direction that fits your current reality.

3

Act

Complete one realistic goal, practice, or community mission.

4

Review

Record what mattered, what you learned, and what opportunity comes next.
