The Fecta loop

One clear step, then review.

Fecta turns personal development into a repeatable process instead of an overwhelming list.

1

Check in

Notice what needs attention in your body, direction, or community.

2

Choose

Pick one meaningful direction that fits your current life.

3

Act

Complete one realistic goal or community mission.

4

Review

Record what changed, keep the growth, and choose what comes next.
