The golden rule

Every conversation ends with a next step

Fecta avoids dumping ten recommendations on the member. It explains why one action fits and lets the member choose another.

Why this step?

Because it fits today

Low energy, 15 minutes, and a goal of becoming more connected. This option is nearby, beginner-friendly, and free.

Built around user control

Simple does not mean restrictive.

Members can customize deeply, but the controls stay out of the way until needed.

AI can be pausedUse Fecta without AI guidance or memory.
Memory needs permissionRemember permanently, temporarily, or not at all.
Modules are optionalAdd Garden, Family, Business, Health, or custom areas.
Settings by conversation“Make text larger.” “Hide Skill Tree.” “Move Community up.”
Location is optionalCommunity features work without precise location.
Data belongs to the memberReview, download, delete, and control what is shared.
